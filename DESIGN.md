# Maflores Tour — Design Foundation

## Source of truth
Active foundation, 2026-07-29. One-page marketing site based on the supplied logo, navy/gold palette, three travel videos, the selected “Expedição Cinematográfica” preview and the agreed form-to-WhatsApp journey.

## Brand and goals
Adventurous, inspiring, trustworthy and human. The business goal is qualified trip requests; the user goal is to feel inspired, understand the personalized service and start planning confidently. This is not a booking engine or fixed-price catalog.

## Audience and journey
Brazilian couples, families and groups planning domestic or international trips. Inspiration → relevant experience → human trust → simple process → qualified form → WhatsApp.

## Principles and visual language
Lead with emotion and prove with clarity. Gold `#F7B51D` calls attention but is never a large background. Navy `#073F56`, deep navy `#032F40`, warm cream `#F7F2E8` and ink `#153744` create trust and warmth. Editorial Georgia-style display type pairs with a neutral sans. Real travel imagery, generous spacing and restrained motion avoid a generic agency template.

## Components
Transparent header, cinematic hero, trust strip, editorial headings, experience columns, split story/video block, process steps, planning form and minimal footer.

## Accessibility and responsive behavior
Semantic landmarks, labels, visible focus, strong contrast, muted autoplay, reduced-motion fallbacks and large touch targets. Desktop uses the horizontal ruins video; mobile uses the vertical airplane video. Grids collapse intentionally below 860px.

## Content voice
Warm, concise and conversational Brazilian Portuguese. Use “você” and “vamos”; avoid pressure, jargon and exaggerated guarantees.

## Constraints and open questions
Vinext/React, one route and compressed local media. The direct WhatsApp number, high-resolution logo, approved testimonials and Instagram handle remain pending; the current WhatsApp action opens a prefilled message without a fixed recipient.
